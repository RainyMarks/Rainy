3. **Redistribute unmodified official builds** verbatim in compliance with

Without prior written permission from RainyMarks, you may **not**:

1. **Keep Rainy branding in modified or re-skinned distributions**: distribute a

   artwork.
2. **Imply official status or endorsement**: present a modified version, fork,
   package, or service as an official RainyMarks release, or imply that

3. **Register confusingly similar marks**: register trademarks, domain names,
   app store listings, or social handles containing `RainyMarks` or confusingly
   similar to `Rainy` for distributing software derived from this project.

## Checklist for Modified Distributions

If you distribute a modified version or derivative work under `GPL-3.0-only`:

- Choose your own distinct product name, executable/installer name, shortcut
  name, and publisher identity.
- Replace the Rainy icons, logos, installer bitmaps, and original pixel cat-ear
  pet artwork with your own assets (or disable those visual assets).
- Keep the required copyright, license, and origin notices (`LICENSE`, `NOTICE`,
  `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`, `LICENSE.upstream`) and clearly indicate that your
  build is a modified version based on Rainy by RainyMarks.
